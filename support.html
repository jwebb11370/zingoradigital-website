Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a bug?

Email support@zingoradigital.com with a description of the issue, your device model, and the operating system version. Screenshots are always helpful.

How do I request a refund?

Refund requests are handled through the store you purchased from — Microsoft Store or Google Play. If you need additional assistance, contact us and we'll help guide you through the process.

How do I transfer my license to a new device?

Contact us at support@zingoradigital.com with your order details and we'll take care of the transfer.

Where is my data stored?

All Zingora Digital apps are designed with privacy first. Your files and personal data are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to external servers without your explicit permission.
